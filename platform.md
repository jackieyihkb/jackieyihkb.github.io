  - title: croFGD

    btn_class: "btn--info"
    excerpt: The Catharanthus roseus functional genomics database. A co-expression network built from transcriptomic data with network search, comparison and analysis, integrating gene family, KEGG pathway, GO term and miRNA information.

Databases are the part of my work with the longest shelf life. A paper describes a
result once; a database keeps answering questions for years after everyone has
forgotten the paper. I build them so that a biologist with no programming
background can get an answer without emailing me first.

<div class="notice--info" markdown="1">
**DOO &mdash; Deep Ocean Omics** &middot; *Nucleic Acids Research* Database Issue, 2026

The most comprehensive multi-omics resource for deep-ocean organisms: 68 species
across seven phyla, 72 genomes, 950 bulk transcriptomes, 15 single-cell
transcriptomes and 1,112 metagenomes, with functional and comparative analysis
toolkits.
