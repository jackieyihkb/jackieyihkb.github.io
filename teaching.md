I teach bioinformatics and data analysis in the
[School of Food and Drug](https://fd.szpu.edu.cn/), Shenzhen Polytechnic
University. Most of my students arrive from a biology or pharmaceutics
background and have never written a line of code, so the courses start from
"what is a file, really" and end with a pipeline that produces a figure you can
put in a report.

Two things I care about in teaching:

- **You should be able to redo it yourself.** Every analysis we run in class is
  something you can re-run at home, on your own laptop, from a documented
  command line &mdash; not a series of clicks nobody remembers.
- **Figures are arguments, not decoration.** A lot of bioinformatics is deciding
  what to plot and what to leave out. I spend real class time on that.

## For prospective students

If you are a student at Shenzhen Polytechnic University and you are interested in
bioinformatics, you do not need to have any programming experience to come and
talk to me &mdash; you need to be willing to be confused for a few weeks. The
usual first step is a small real project on public data: pick an organism, pick a
question, and build something that answers it. Write to
[jiajie@szpu.edu.cn](mailto:jiajie@szpu.edu.cn) and tell me what you would like
to work on.
