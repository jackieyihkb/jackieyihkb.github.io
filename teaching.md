I teach in the [School of Food and Drug](https://fd.szpu.edu.cn/), Shenzhen
Polytechnic University. The courses start from the practical basics, such as
what a file actually is and how to read a command line, and end with a pipeline
that produces a figure a student can defend.

Two principles shape how I teach:

- **Every analysis should be reproducible by the student.** Anything we run in
  class can be re-run at home, on a laptop, from a documented command line.
- **Figures are arguments, not decoration.** A large part of bioinformatics is
  deciding what to plot and what to leave out, and we spend real class time on
  that decision.

## Courses

<h3 class="course-title">生物信息学基础实训</h3>

<p class="course-meta"><em>Bioinformatics Practical Training</em>, undergraduate practical course, <span class="nowrap">2026–2027</span></p>

Students collect their own saliva samples and follow the question from sample to
interpretation: DNA preparation, the laboratory assay and the bioinformatics
analysis of alcohol-metabolism variants. Because each student is answering a
question about themselves, every wet-lab step and every command has a concrete
purpose.

For the current syllabus, lecture slides or assignment details, please
[email me](mailto:jiajie@szpu.edu.cn).

## For prospective students

If you are a student at Shenzhen Polytechnic University and you are curious
about bioinformatics, you do not need prior programming experience to come and
talk to me. The usual first step is a small project on public data: choose an
organism, choose a question, and build something that answers it. Write to
[jiajie@szpu.edu.cn](mailto:jiajie@szpu.edu.cn) with a sentence about what you
would like to work on.
